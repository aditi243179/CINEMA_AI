FAQ

Common Questions

Is CinemaAI really free? +
Yes — completely. No credit card required. The free plan gives you 10 video generations per day, 1080p HD export with no watermark, and access to 30+ VFX effects and all 50 camera presets. Most creators never need to upgrade.
How long does video generation take? +
Most videos generate in 20–60 seconds, depending on length and complexity. Pro users get priority queue access for even faster results. We also support concurrent job processing on Creator and Pro plans.
Can I use videos commercially? +
Free and Creator plan videos can be used for personal and social media content. The Pro plan includes full commercial licensing, making it ideal for ad campaigns, client projects, and brand content.
Which AI models do you use? +
We support OpenAI Sora 2, Google Veo 3 (with native audio), and our own proprietary CinemaAI DOP model optimized for cinematic camera work. Pro users can switch between models per generation.
What formats can I export in? +
Export in MP4, MOV, and WebM. Resolution options: 720p (free), 1080p HD (free), 4K (Creator+), 8K (Pro). All exports are optimized for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and professional production.
Is there an API for developers? +
Yes! The CinemaAI API is available on the Pro plan. Integrate video generation, image creation, and VFX effects directly into your own apps and workflows via our RESTful API with full documentation.
